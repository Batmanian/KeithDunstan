The founder of the Collingwood Tote was John Wren, and in many ways he was something of a wonder himself. He did not smoke, he did not drink and he had a tenacity of purpose that was awesome to behold. John Wren was born in Ballarat Street, Collingwood, in 1871. He started work wheeling firewood at a woodyard and his next job was at a boot factory. One of his favourite sayings was: 'Believe me. I knew what it was to feel the pinch in my youth. I had very little to do to feel the pitch in my reach.'

Wren worked at Whybourn's boot plant, and his future did not seem brilliant. In Collingwood they had their fathers into the factories.
