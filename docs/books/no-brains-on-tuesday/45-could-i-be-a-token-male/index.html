Could I Be a Token Male?

3 min read.

First published 1991

Prompted by the news that female committee membership is now compulsory, a look at Edmund Cooper's novel in which by 2043 the ratio of females to males is twelve to five and men are needed only for stud purposes, with a plea that when the position is reversed the powerful female prime ministers will appoint a token male to a board here and there.

Some of my best friends are women.

Indeed, if you cross-examine and point a strong light in my face, I would say something even more outrageous. I actually prefer women.

It is not generally realised the incredible advance to power they have made. It is interesting that rising female power has become the subject of a famous novel which has gone to many editions: Five to One by Edmund Cooper.

This book is set early in the 21st century. The pill took over completely. Marriage no longer became a social necessity. Before long, females were more socially desirable than males. In the year 2043 the ratio of females to males was twelve to five.

Males were necessary only for stud procreation purposes.

The women all used longevity pills, which meant they had a long-life species. The pills did have an awkward side effect — they tended to cause nymphomania. So the poor fellow Dion, a poet turned male prostitute who decides to fight back, is overworked.

And it was quite fascinating: as the females became dominant in the community, they became more powerful physically. Already I have noticed that when I am jogging the women go past me with the speed of light.

I am not suggesting that all this is certain to happen. But I am humbly suggesting that we are not quite what the talking female tokens believe. I know it is five to twelve now; it could be a quarter to eleven.

I am sorry to report that now, apparently, it is compulsory to have women members of committees. The Minister gave them positions only where men were still in command, but he has established a precedent. The next Government will have four women; the one after that will have five, and so on.

A similar situation applies. Government has to have its appointed females.

The very week we were discussing the tokenising of females, we had the awesome news of female power from Los Angeles. Peter Holm, now separated from Joan Collins, was claiming his $17,000 a month at the divorce trial.

I do hope that when the position is reversed, the powerful female prime ministers will make cabinet kind enough to appoint a token male.

And when twelve o'clock comes I want it remembered that I preferred females and I would like to be appointed to a board here and there as the token male.
