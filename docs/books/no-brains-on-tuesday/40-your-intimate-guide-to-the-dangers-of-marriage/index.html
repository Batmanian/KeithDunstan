Your Intimate Guide to the Dangers of Marriage

3 min read.

First published 1991

A Monash University survey finding 80 per cent of families fight over breakfast prompts a rival independent survey introducing the Marital Stress Factor scale, rating garden disagreements at MSF68, picture framing at MSF65, watching football at MSF80, going out to parties at MSF72 and keeping the house in show condition for five minutes before guests arrive as the most awesome moment of all.

Columns Which Deal with Domestic Bliss

You will have noticed that an extraordinary survey has come out of Monash University.

Professor Maurice Balson has shown that more than 80 per cent of families fight over breakfast.

Admittedly breakfast is a sensitive time but at our house we have found other occasions that are even more lethal.

So on your behalf this column has spent the past week making its own independent survey. First it was necessary to invent a unit of scale, which we will call MSF, Marital Stress Factor.

If we take 100 per cent MSF as finding your spouse in bed with another, then we grade all MSFs back from that.

We found that the two worst ages for children are two and 17. These are the worst ages when offspring are totally uncontrollable and when getting them to be told anything causes conflict. Parents spend as much time as unpaid taxi drivers. MSF80.

A warning of the most potent kind: the spouse who always loses the way, showing inability to map read, appointments already past. MSF40.

As for domestic sport, it should be perfectly pleasant along with your spouse if you enjoy it together: mixed doubles MSF65. Golf MSF75. However, it is only dangerous if you don't do it together. Fishing widow or widower MSF80. Golf widower MSF93.

There are MSFs that have to be watched very carefully. The garden is far more dangerous than wives generally realise. Have you noticed how husbands and wives can never agree on the placement of an azalea, callistemon, or camellia? Once one has the treasure in position, inevitably it has to be moved two metres to the right. Or if it doesn't, really it should have been moved the other way. MSF68.

Picture framing too has to be watched. The Arthur Boyd looks ridiculous beside the Leila Counihan. If it were six inches higher, and now three inches to the left, you would get more balance. MSF65.

Oddly enough television is not a cause of marital stress, except in getting children to stop watching it. Our survey found that 90 per cent of wives and 60 per cent of husbands sleep through most programs. MSF22.

The telephone is an MSF cause. When apart, spouses need to communicate at least hourly. Otherwise there are questions as to what the other is up to. Failure to do so: MSF30. At worst, MSF90.

Then it is clearly understood that the highest MSFs involve both going out to parties and having your own. There is the MSF: 'You accepted the invitation — I didn't. Normally I wouldn't go to their place in a fit!' MSF72. 'Now I got called after and should have declined half an hour ago.' MSF75. 'Are you ready? Get ready.' MSF variable but steep.

But even more delicate is having your own party. Nothing quite equals the MSF in arranging the guest list, and we found remarkable consensus on this. The most awesome time, when the stress is so lovely it is almost beautiful, is the five minutes before the guests are due to arrive.
