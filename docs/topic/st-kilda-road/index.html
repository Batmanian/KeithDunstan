“St Kilda Rd — or Disneyland Modern”

Published 08 Sep 1962

Laments the once-grand St Kilda Road's decline into brash "Disneyland Modern" office architecture, then reports on the inaugural meeting of Melbourne's new Noise Abatement Society, modelled on "America's quietest city," Memphis.
