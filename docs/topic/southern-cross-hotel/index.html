Topic: Southern Cross Hotel

10 items include the topic of Southern Cross Hotel.

The Southern Cross Hotel was Melbourne's first newly built hotel in decades when it opened in 1962 on the old Eastern Market site, a Miami-style Pan-American venture Keith Dunstan toured for its novelties, Australia's first hotel bidet among them, right down to a bowling alley built over a bank vault.
