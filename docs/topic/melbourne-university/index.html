Topic: Melbourne University

7 items include the topic of Melbourne University.

The University of Melbourne is Victoria's oldest university, whose students led a determined, months-long 1962 campaign against the Bolte government over the hanging of Robert Tait that Keith Dunstan covered for the Bulletin.
