Topic: Echuca

8 items include the topic of Echuca.

Echuca is the Murray River port town in northern Victoria that was Australia's greatest inland port during the paddlesteamer era, its historic wharf and waterfront precinct a magnet for heritage tourism from the 1970s.

Published 07 Mar 1970
