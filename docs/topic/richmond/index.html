Richmond is the inner eastern Melbourne suburb on the Yarra, home of Squizzy Taylor and a working-class heartland.

“Yarra Bursts its Banks”

Published 1988

The Yarra floods Melbourne in October 1844 after two days of north-easterly rain, leaving Flinders Street and the wharves under water, boats plying between houses, and citizens heaping anathemas on a Town Council that never built an embankment.

Published 1988

Melbourne gangster Squizzy Taylor and his rival 'Snowy' Cutmore shoot each other dead in a Carlton bedroom in October 1927, the bloody end of a long vendetta, and the newspapers line up to call Taylor a swaggering criminal fop with the cowardice of a sneak.

Published 1988

Billy Hughes has Labor's Hugh Mahon expelled from the House of Representatives in November 1920, the only member ever thrown out, after Mahon told a Richmond rally that the death of hunger-striker Terence MacSwiney would one day shake the foundations of 'this bloody and accursed Empire'.
