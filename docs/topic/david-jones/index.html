Topic: David Jones

5 items include the topic of David Jones.

David Jones is Australia's oldest department store chain, founded in Sydney in 1838, whose scale Keith Dunstan invoked alongside Coles Myer as the kind of corporate giant a scrappy small shop could only dream of rivalling.

Welcomes Barry Humphries home from London for a nine-night Assembly Hall season, recounting his elaborate public pranks, including the "Ashburton Line" breakfast-in-a-carriage stunt and slices of ham hidden in a David Jones bargain sale.
