Topic: Sydney Opera House

4 items include the topic of Sydney Opera House.

The Sydney Opera House was still under construction through the years Keith Dunstan wrote his Bulletin column, providing a ready punchline whenever Melbourne's own civic ambitions, like the proposed Coal Exchange rotunda, needed deflating by comparison.
