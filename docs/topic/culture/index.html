“John Bull's vineyard born anew”

Published 28 Aug 1984

Victoria once called itself "John Bull's Vineyard" and in 1894–95 had 12,265 hectares under vine; phylloxera, depression and wowserism nearly wiped it out. Published 18 Sep 1984

Melbourne is losing its famous herd instinct, VFL crowds are down 7.6 percent and 240,000 on 1983, harness racing averages have slumped from 23,400 (1947–48) to 8,200, and greyhound attendance fell 33 percent between 1977 and 1983. “Melbourne agonises over its super Moomba”

Published 06 Nov 1984

Victoria's 150th birthday celebrations begin on November 17, but reaching this point has been agonising. MacPherson Robertson gave £140,000 for the centenary in 1934, funding the MacRobertson Girls High School, a bridge and an air race. The 150th started under Sir John Holland, survived a government change and board spill, then under Ranald Macdonald shaped into 26 hectares of Westgate Park, a Gallery of Sport at the MCG, Spoleto festival for Melbourne in October 1985, the Great Victorian Bike Ride, and world championship cricket under lights.
