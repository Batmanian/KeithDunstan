Topic: Mornington Peninsula

8 items include the topic of Mornington Peninsula.

The Mornington Peninsula is the broad peninsula south-east of Melbourne enclosing Port Phillip Bay's eastern shore, whose beaches, fishing grounds and scallop beds feature in Keith Dunstan's writing from the 1960s seafood boom to his own later vineyard.

“Mud Crabs for Boys, Scallops for All”

Published 27 Jun 1964

Melbourne has become the headquarters of a booming scallop industry, with 90 boats working Port Phillip Bay and five million scallops a month being dredged, a transformation Keith Dunstan celebrates as one of the best things to happen to the city since Baron von Mueller designed the Botanic Gardens.

A tour of American-financed cattle stations on Cape York Peninsula, where Sir William Gunn's Gunn Rural Management and the King Ranch operation at Tully were fencing scrubmaster herds, breeding Brahmans and Santa Gertrudis, and bulldozing rainforest for legume pasture.
