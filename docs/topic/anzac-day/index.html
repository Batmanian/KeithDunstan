Topic: Anzac Day

4 items include the topic of Anzac Day.

Anzac Day, held every 25 April, commemorates the 1915 Gallipoli landing and Australia's war dead; for Keith Dunstan's family it was a fraught annual ritual, the one day his Victoria Cross-winning father allowed himself to remember, and briefly relive, the horror of Lone Pine.

“Australian Myths”

Published 1972

Examines the knocking of Australia's most sacred national myths, from the vicious attacks on Ned Kelly in Melbourne Punch and the homosexuality allegation that proved the one knock too far, through the sustained persecution of Les Darcy as a wartime shirker, the failed attempts to deflate Don Bradman and Kingsford Smith, and the long campaign against the Sydney Harbour Bridge and the Melbourne Shrine of Remembrance, ending with the Anzac Day controversies of the 1960s.
