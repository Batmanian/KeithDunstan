Topic: Sir Donald Bradman

14 items include the topic of Sir Donald Bradman.

Sir Donald Bradman, Australia's greatest batsman, had a Test batting average of 99.94, the sport's most celebrated statistic, and appears throughout Keith Dunstan's writing both as the ultimate shorthand for sporting greatness and in his administrative role as chairman of the Australian Cricket Board of Control in the early 1960s, defending Test cricket's dignity against Henry Bolte's comic Centenary replay scheme and, in 1971, cancelling South Africa's tour.
