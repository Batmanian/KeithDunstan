Topic: Yarra River

6 items include the topic of Yarra River.

The Yarra River winds through the centre of Melbourne, the site of Keith Dunstan's civic dreams of a Melbourne answering to Paris's Seine or Venice's canals, and of the traffic-clogged freeway overflow that pushed into once-exclusive Toorak.
