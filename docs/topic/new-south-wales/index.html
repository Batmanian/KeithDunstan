Topic: New South Wales

31 items include the topic of New South Wales.

New South Wales is Australia's oldest and most populous state, whose old rivalry with Victoria over everything from air routes to beer supply Keith Dunstan mined for comedy throughout his Bulletin column, most explicitly in his tongue-in-cheek 1962 peace plan for 'NSW-Vic co-existence'.
