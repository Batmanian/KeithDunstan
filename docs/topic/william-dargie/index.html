Topic: William Dargie

4 items include the topic of William Dargie.

William Dargie (1912-2003) was a Melbourne portrait painter and eight-time winner of the Archibald Prize, a traditionalist who clashed publicly with modernist National Gallery director Eric Westbrook, wore white tie to the 1968 opening of the National Gallery of Victoria, and was among the distinguished members of Hector Crawford's celebrated Sunday tennis club.
