Topic: Harold Holt

12 items include the topic of Harold Holt.

Harold Holt was the Liberal Prime Minister from 1966 until his presumed drowning off Cheviot Beach, Portsea, in December 1967, just three months after Keith Dunstan's Anti-Football League gave him its inaugural Wilkie Medal for never once being seen at a football match.

Published 12 May 1962

On the eve of his release after 12 years, revisits Melbourne's most sensational murder case, the conviction of the charming, well-connected John Bryan Kerr for the 1949 strangling of Elizabeth Maureen Williams, and the newspaper campaign that fought to reopen it.
