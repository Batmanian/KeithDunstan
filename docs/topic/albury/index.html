Topic: Albury

12 items include the topic of Albury.

Albury is the New South Wales town on the Murray River where Melbourne's and Sydney's differently gauged rail lines first met in 1883, forcing generations of interstate travellers to change trains there, and which Keith Dunstan used as shorthand for the old Sydney-Melbourne rivalry, once floating it as the site for a summit between premiers Bolte and Heffron.
