Topic: Qantas

9 items include the topic of Qantas.

Qantas is Australia's national airline, whose domestic route politics fed the Sydney-Melbourne jetport rivalry Keith Dunstan mined for comedy, and which also carried Barry Humphries home from London for the homecoming season Keith covered in 1962.

“Return of Norm and Edna Everage”

Published 04 Aug 1962

Welcomes Barry Humphries home from London for a nine-night Assembly Hall season, recounting his elaborate public pranks, including the "Ashburton Line" breakfast-in-a-carriage stunt and slices of ham hidden in a David Jones bargain sale.
