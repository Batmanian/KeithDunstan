Topic: Joan Sutherland

9 items include the topic of Joan Sutherland.

Joan Sutherland was the Australian soprano nicknamed 'La Stupenda', world famous for her bel canto roles including the Mad Scene from 'Lucia di Lammermoor', a showstopper Keith Dunstan invoked as the gold standard of operatic sound he'd love a universal remote to summon on command.
