Topic: Melbourne Grammar

8 items include the topic of Melbourne Grammar.

Melbourne Grammar is one of the city's oldest and most prestigious private schools, whose Wadhurst preparatory campus gave the young Keith Dunstan his first miserable taste of formal education before he was sent on to board at Geelong Grammar, and which later produced Barry Humphries and the actor Frank Thring.

“The Passion at School”

Published 1973

Surveys the near-religious grip of school sport on Australian boarding and grammar schools, from the ritual status of football and rowing colours to the pitched battles between Melbourne Grammar and Wesley College, and closes on the modern anxiety of children's sport turning parents into fanatics.
