Topic: Melbourne City Council

7 items include the topic of Melbourne City Council.

The Melbourne City Council administered the city's 1889 hydraulic lift network, operating 20 miles of cast iron pipe under the CBD at 800 pounds per square inch until a 1962 compensation payout for a burst-pipe flood convinced the Council to shut the whole system down.
