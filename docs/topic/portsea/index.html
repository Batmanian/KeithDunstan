Topic: Portsea

11 items include the topic of Portsea.

Portsea is the exclusive holiday township at the tip of the Mornington Peninsula, long a magnet for Melbourne's wealthy summer set, and home to the Portsea Camp where athletics coach Percy Cerutty put Herb Elliott through his punishing training regime.
