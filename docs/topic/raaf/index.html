Topic: RAAF

11 items include the topic of RAAF.

The Royal Australian Air Force was the service Keith Dunstan joined at eighteen in 1943, training as a pilot instead of the wireless air gunner he'd been assigned to become thanks to his father's string-pulling, an episode he recounted at length in 'No Brains at All'.

A profile of the RAAF's officer training academy at Point Cook, tracing the base's history from Sir Richard Williams's first flight in 1914 to the newly degree-granting RAAF Academy of 1964, where cadets study physics alongside their flying.
