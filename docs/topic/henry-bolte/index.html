Topic: Henry Bolte

24 items include the topic of Henry Bolte.

Sir Henry Bolte was Victoria's blunt, long-serving Liberal Premier from 1955 to 1972, a plain-spoken figure Keith Dunstan needled throughout the Bulletin years, from his government's standoff with students over the hanging of Robert Tait to the misquoted line that earned him the nickname 'Satchmo'.
