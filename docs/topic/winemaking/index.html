Topic: Winemaking

6 items include the topic of Winemaking.

Winemaking became Keith Dunstan's own late-life trade when he and Marie planted a small vineyard on the Mornington Peninsula, an unglamorous slog of pruning, netting, spraying and waiting he chronicled, warts and all, in 'My Life with the Demon'.

Published 1994
