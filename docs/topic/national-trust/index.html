Topic: National Trust

7 items include the topic of National Trust.

The National Trust (Victoria) is the organisation that advocates for the preservation of places of historical significance, which opened the Nareeb estate in Toorak for public inspection in 1964 before its auction, classifying it as interesting with preservation desirable, a rating that proved insufficient to save it from subdivision.

“You Need More Than a 'D' in Toorak”

Published 26 Dec 1964

The opening of 'Nareeb', Toorak's last great private estate, for public auction draws 15,000 visitors to the 53-acre Kooyong Road property unchanged since 1888, where the contents, from a 1920 Rolls-Royce to aspidistras, sell for over £18,000.
