Topic: Canberra

12 items include the topic of Canberra.

Canberra is Australia's purpose-built national capital, whose founding King O'Malley championed with fruit-cake oratory and which Keith Dunstan visited on assignment during Royal Tours, complete with buildings painted only on the side the Queen would see.
