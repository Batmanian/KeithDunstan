Topic: Jeff Hook

7 items include the topic of Jeff Hook.

Jeff Hook was the Sun News-Pictorial's cartoonist and a long-time colleague of Keith Dunstan's, a Tasmanian who signed his sketches 'Jeff' with a tiny hidden hook and once out-sketched a Boeing factory tour guide with a single ten-minute drawing.
