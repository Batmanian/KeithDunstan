Topic: Geelong Grammar

13 items include the topic of Geelong Grammar.

Geelong Grammar is the elite Anglican boarding school on Victoria's Bellarine Peninsula where Keith Dunstan boarded from 1939 through a rigid prefect hierarchy at Francis Brown House, and which decades later became briefly famous as the school Prince Charles attended at its bush campus, Timbertop.
