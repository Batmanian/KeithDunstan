Topic: Collingwood

9 items include the topic of Collingwood.

Collingwood is the working-class inner-Melbourne suburb and one of the VFL's oldest, most fiercely supported football clubs, part of the all-consuming football culture Keith Dunstan surveyed with equal parts affection and exasperation before eventually founding the Anti-Football League.
