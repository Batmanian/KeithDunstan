Topic: Collingwood

13 items include the topic of Collingwood.

Collingwood is the working-class inner-Melbourne suburb and one of the VFL's oldest, most fiercely supported football clubs, part of the all-consuming football culture Keith Dunstan surveyed with equal parts affection and exasperation before eventually founding the Anti-Football League.

“Football and TV”

Published 17 Mar 1962

Covers Melbourne's fierce row over the VFL's ban on televising the last quarter of League football, then Victoria's new tallest building, the 24-storey Consolidated Zinc, opening almost unnoticed.
