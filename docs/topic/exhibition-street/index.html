Topic: Exhibition Street

4 items include the topic of Exhibition Street.

Exhibition Street is one of the Melbourne CBD's main thoroughfares, home in the early 1960s to the newly built Southern Cross Hotel that Keith Dunstan toured for its novelties on opening.

Published 31 Mar 1962

Previews Pan-American's new Miami-style Southern Cross Hotel rising on the old Eastern Market site, then the Lord Mayor's £770 replacement robes after the old ones, handed down for decades, finally fell apart.
