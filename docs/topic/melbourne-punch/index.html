Topic: Melbourne Punch

18 items include the topic of Melbourne Punch.

Melbourne Punch was a nineteenth-century satirical weekly whose jab at a citizenry too absorbed in sport to care about Federation ('He can only watch one game at a time') Keith Dunstan quoted as proof the national sporting obsession pre-dated the twentieth century.
