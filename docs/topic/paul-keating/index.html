Topic: Paul Keating

7 items include the topic of Paul Keating.

Paul Keating was the former Labor Prime Minister renowned for his gift for vivid, cutting insults, whom Keith Dunstan nominated, tongue firmly in cheek, to chair an inquiry into supplying Australians with fresh new swear words to replace their worn-out old ones.

“Would a Tax on the Waistline Help?”

Published 1991

A detailed proposal for NICK, the New Inspired Consumption Kill, as a more rational alternative to the consumption tax, targeting automobiles for their emissions, graffiti pens, plastic bags, outdoor advertising, noisy motorbikes, television repeats, corporate take-over bids and the booming diet industry.

“The Intelligent Man's Budget Guide”

Published 1991

An A-to-Z economic dictionary for the bewildered citizen, defining Accord, Deficit, Gearing, J-curve, Pipeline and Zero in terms of actual Australian experience under Hawke, Keating and Kirner rather than Treasury optimism.

“A Prostate Could Make You AA”

Published 1991

The indignities of the modern credit-rating system, from Moody's down-grading the State Bank and a credit card application requiring a month-long inquisition, to an automatic teller swallowing a card and a South Yarra liquor store refusing a cheque, culminating in the observation that Bob Hawke's prostate operation will surely restore his public approval rating to Aaa.

The VFL's decision to hand out retroactive Brownlow Medals to players who tied in the past prompts a suggestion that the same principle be applied to all Australian life, with a full list of those who should receive belated honours, concluding with shifting Bob Hawke to the House of Lords.
