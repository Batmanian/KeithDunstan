Topic: Dick Hamer

15 items include the topic of Dick Hamer.

Dick Hamer (Rupert James Hamer, 1916-2004) was Premier of Victoria from 1972 to 1981, a liberal-minded Liberal leader who introduced the 'Garden State' branding that Keith Dunstan recorded as the civic ambition of the Hamer era.

Published 05 Jun 1971

A report on opening night of the musical 'Hair' at Melbourne's Metro Theatre, where the audience dressed as gurus and swamis outnumbered ordinary patrons, Ron Barassi attended in the front row, and Harry Miller's post-show mystery buses toured the city before arriving at a whitewashed Fitzroy dairy showing Judy Garland films and Carmen Miranda.
