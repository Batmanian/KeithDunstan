Topic: Sydney University

5 items include the topic of Sydney University.

The University of Sydney is Australia's oldest university, founded in 1850, whose anatomy department kept the Pyjama Girl's body in a formalin bath for a decade.

“Who is the Pyjama Girl?”

Published 1988

The battered, half-burned body of a young woman in canary-coloured silk pyjamas is found in a culvert outside Albury in September 1934, beginning the Pyjama Girl mystery that kept her body in a formalin bath for ten years before it was identified as Linda Agostini and her husband convicted of manslaughter.

“Navy Captures Rabaul”

Published 1988

Australian forces seize Rabaul and German New Guinea in September 1914, the nation's first serious engagement of the First World War, in which Captain Brian Pockley became one of the first Australians killed after giving his Red Cross brassard to the stoker carrying a wounded sailor.

“Atomic Explosion at Monte Bello Islands”

Published 1988

Britain explodes its first atomic bomb in the Monte Bello Islands off Western Australia in October 1952, rattling windows in Onslow, delighting a London press crowing about 'Our Bomb', and narrowly losing a race with the birth of the town's 'atomic baby'.

“Hargrave takes Flight”

Published 1988

Lawrence Hargrave lifts himself 16 feet off the beach at Stanwell Park under a string of four box kites in November 1894, proving his curved-wing lifting surfaces while Sydney University's chancellor insists men will never fly.
