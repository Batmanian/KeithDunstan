Topic: Menzies Hotel

12 items include the topic of Menzies Hotel.

Menzies Hotel, at the corner of Bourke and William Streets, was one of Melbourne's most illustrious hotels, remembered by Keith Dunstan for its separate 'Governor's entrance' so that vice-regal visitors need never be seen walking through the front door of a licensed premises.
