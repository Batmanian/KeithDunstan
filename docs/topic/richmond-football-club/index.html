Topic: Richmond Football Club

15 items include the topic of Richmond Football Club.

Richmond Football Club, the Tigers, is one of the oldest clubs in the Victorian Football League; Keith Dunstan cited the case of a devoted Richmond supporter who requested his ashes be buried in the goal square at the Richmond football ground, a wish fulfilled by club secretary Alan Schwab.
