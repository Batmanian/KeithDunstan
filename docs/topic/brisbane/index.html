Topic: Brisbane

5 items include the topic of Brisbane.

Brisbane is the Queensland capital where Keith Dunstan spent the mid-1950s as a columnist on the Courier-Mail after editor Colin Bednall recruited him from Melbourne, in what was then a slow-paced, sub-tropical city of half a million with a marked inferiority complex about the southern states.

“From Prickly Pear to Petroleum”

Published 01 Jul 1963

A visit to the Queensland oil boomtowns of Tara and Moonie, tracing the district's turn from a prickly-pear-choked backwater once known as 'The Lost Province' into the birthplace of Australia's oil industry, and meeting the selection farmers whose land the oil was found under.
