Topic: Surfers Paradise

8 items include the topic of Surfers Paradise.

Surfers Paradise is the Gold Coast resort strip that boomed through the 1950s and 60s on Miami-style tower blocks and invented traditions like a water-skiing Father Christmas, and whose local council was first to approve the bikini for its beaches in 1955, all of which Keith Dunstan surveyed with amused disdain in his Walkabout travel writing.
