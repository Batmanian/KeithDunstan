Topic: Dame Nellie Melba

7 items include the topic of Dame Nellie Melba.

Dame Nellie Melba was the Australian operatic soprano whose international fame in the early twentieth century made her, in Keith Dunstan's estimation, one of the few Australians ever more famous at home and abroad than the subjects of his own later profiles.

“Clara Bow, Ronald Colman and All That”

Published 27 Jul 1963

Marks Hoyts' golden jubilee against the demolition of Melbourne's old Savoy Theatre, tracing the cinema chain's rise from a Bourke Street dentist's magic-lantern hobby to the picture-palace rivalry between the Hoyts Regent and its rival, the State, and the wave of name changes that followed as the old chains contracted under television.

“The Hot Dog is Still Too Thin / Radio Australia: The World's Most Successful”

Published 19 Dec 1964

On the eve of Radio Australia's 25th birthday, a visit to its cramped Melbourne headquarters reveals the world's most-listened-to overseas radio service, ahead of the BBC and Voice of America, sustained by a quarter-million letters a year from listeners across Asia.

Published 1972
