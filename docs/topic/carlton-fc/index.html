“Colour blind to the grim facts”

Published 23 Jun 1973

Melbourne's commercial dream of selling objects in football club colours always fails, from Gas and Fuel Corporation porcelain stoves in all 12 club colours to Myers' wash-and-wear synthetic wigs at $6.50, while Keith Dunstan's 43-year survey of attendance figures reveals VFL crowds have fallen from nine percent of Melbourne's population in 1930 to only 5.3 percent today.

“A new weapon in the beer war”

Published 25 Aug 1973

Courage Brewery's latest gambit in its disappointing campaign against Carlton is to put Brownlow Medal contenders' faces on aluminium cans, but after $5 million in accumulated losses and a series of failed products from Crest to Colonial Ale to Tankard beer, Keith Dunstan suggests they would do better decorating cans with writers, since almost all great Australian writers drink awfully well.

“Last of the great footy finals”

Published 29 Sep 1973

With the VFL's contract with the MCG expired, the question is whether the grand final will eventually move to Waverley Park, which opened in 1970 and will hold 80,000 by next year but can only be reached by car through catastrophic traffic jams, while the MCG remains one of the five biggest grounds in the world and the only place where a human being can have the opportunity of being hooted by 120,000.
