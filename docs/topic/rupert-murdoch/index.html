Topic: Rupert Murdoch

7 items include the topic of Rupert Murdoch.

Rupert Murdoch built the global News Corporation empire from a single inherited Adelaide newspaper, and by the early 2010s was predicting the death of print newspapers by 2020, a forecast Keith Dunstan, writing amid the wreckage of the News of the World scandal, refused to believe.
