Topic: Carlton and United Breweries

11 items include the topic of Carlton and United Breweries.

Carlton and United Breweries was Victoria's dominant brewing company, formed by an early-twentieth-century merger and long the state's de facto beer monopoly under its autocratic managing director Reg 'Foge' Fogarty, whose grip on price and supply Keith Dunstan chronicled in both the Bulletin and 'My Life with the Demon'.
