Topic: St Kilda

28 items include the topic of St Kilda.

St Kilda is the beachside inner-Melbourne suburb whose once-grand boulevard, St Kilda Road, Keith Dunstan watched give way to brash 1960s office architecture he dubbed 'Disneyland Modern', even as the suburb's seedier corners supplied him with stories of illegal SP bookmaking penthouses.
