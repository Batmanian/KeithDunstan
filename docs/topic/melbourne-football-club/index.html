Topic: Melbourne Football Club

12 items include the topic of Melbourne Football Club.

The Melbourne Football Club, nicknamed the Demons, is the oldest football club in the world, founded in 1858; Keith Dunstan noted Walter Hauser played in its reserves ruck before a knee injury ended his prospects, and later cited the club's sacking of coach Dean Bailey as fresh ammunition for his Anti-Football League.
