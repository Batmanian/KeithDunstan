Topic: Fosters Lager

7 items include the topic of Fosters Lager.

Foster's Lager is Australia's best-known beer export, a Carlton and United Breweries product whose supply and pricing Keith Dunstan tracked through decades of Melbourne pub life, from Premier Henry Bolte's 'Satchmo' nickname to the wartime origins of the six o'clock swill.
