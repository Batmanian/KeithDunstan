Topic: Burke and Wills

5 items include the topic of Burke and Wills.

Robert O'Hara Burke and William John Wills led the ill-fated 1860-61 Victorian Exploring Expedition across the continent from Melbourne to the Gulf of Carpentaria and back, both dying on the return journey, a disaster Keith Dunstan invoked both as the pair's forlorn statue at Collins and Swanston Streets and as a benchmark of endurance for the masochistic long-distance cyclists of his own day.

“Burke and Wills Perish, but Archer Wins the Cup”

Published 1988

In the week Melbourne learns that Burke and Wills have died, the Victoria Turf Club runs the first Melbourne Cup in November 1861, won by six lengths by the Sydney horse Archer after a fall that killed the mare Medora and broke a jockey's arm.
