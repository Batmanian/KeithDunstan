Topic: Australian Labor Party

7 items include the topic of Australian Labor Party.

The Australian Labor Party is one of Australia's two major political parties, founded in 1891 from the trade union movement, which Keith Dunstan covered as a political reporter through the Menzies, Holt, Gorton, and McMahon years.

“Could Enzymes Lift That Veil off Dull Elections?”

Published 09 Aug 1969

The proliferation of mystery ingredients in household products — Puradent, Hexachlorophene, Gardol, Enzolve, Politogene — leads Keith Dunstan to propose that the Labor, Country, and Liberal parties adopt the same scientific marketing language for the coming election, with Politogene, deep-acting enzymes, and OK-100-15 as their respective selling points.
