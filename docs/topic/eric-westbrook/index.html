Topic: Eric Westbrook

6 items include the topic of Eric Westbrook.

Eric Westbrook directed Melbourne's National Gallery of Victoria through the 1960s, championing modernist painters against traditionalists like William Dargie in a public letters-page feud Keith Dunstan covered, and later oversaw the gallery's move into its new St Kilda Road building.
