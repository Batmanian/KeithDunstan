Topic: Anti-Football League

4 items include the topic of Anti-Football League.

The Anti Football League was a tongue-in-cheek club Keith Dunstan founded in his newspaper column in 1967 for Melburnians weary of the city's football obsession, which went on to raise large sums for charity through stunts like anti-Grand-Final tickets and its annual Wilkie Medal.
