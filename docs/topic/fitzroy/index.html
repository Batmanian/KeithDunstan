Topic: Fitzroy

11 items include the topic of Fitzroy.

Fitzroy is the inner Melbourne suburb immediately north-east of the city, historically working-class and a byword for the opposite of Toorak's polish, which Keith Dunstan used as the backdrop for a joyriding car crash and brawl reported live on radio station 3UZ's Sunday-morning 'Newsbeat'.

Published 14 Jul 1962

An affectionate dissection of Toorak's century of unrivalled Melbourne snob value, from its jealously small postcode SE2 to its chauffeur-driven Rolls Royces and Tudor-revival Village shops.
