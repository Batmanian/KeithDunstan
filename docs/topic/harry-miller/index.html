Topic: Harry Miller

6 items include the topic of Harry Miller.

Harry Miller was an Australian theatrical producer who brought 'Hair,' 'Grease' and other productions to Melbourne in the early 1970s, known for extravagant first-night parties with champagne buses, secret destinations and hot dogs alongside 'Harry-M-burgers,' and for making St Paul's Cathedral into what Keith Dunstan said was becoming as good an entertainment venue as any hard-top Hoyts.

“Middle-Aged and Methodist Amidst the Nakedness”

Published 14 Jun 1969

The Melbourne opening of the musical Hair, after producer Harry Miller won approval from Chief Secretary Willis to stage it with full nudity intact, prompts Keith Dunstan's report on the audience in fur and chainmail, the show's sex, blasphemy, and four-letter words assessed from a Methodist-raised vantage point, and the aftermath party at the Chevron where Graham Kennedy's gift of a Rolls-Royce was the talk of the night.

“Where the Aim Is Big and the Licence Longest”

Published 28 Jun 1969

A Queen's Birthday weekend visit to Thredbo, the $44 million Lend Lease ski resort developed by G. J. Dusseldorp in the New South Wales Snowy Mountains, finds wall-to-wall carpet, heated swimming pools, R-and-R packages for Vietnam servicemen, and an Alpine Hotel holding the only liquor licence on the mountain, positioned so that everyone must funnel through it to reach the chairlift.

Published 05 Jun 1971
