Topic: John Pascoe Fawkner

3 items include the topic of John Pascoe Fawkner.

John Pascoe Fawkner was one of the founders of Melbourne, a newspaper proprietor and politician who became a central figure in the Melbourne-versus-Sydney rivalry that Keith Dunstan traced from the colony's earliest days in 'Knockers'.

“The First Cricket Matches”

Published 1962

Follows the Melbourne Cricket Club's earliest contests, its lean years, the celebrations of Victoria's separation and the colony's first intercolonial matches against Van Diemen's Land.

Published 1972
