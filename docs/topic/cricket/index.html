Topic: Cricket

17 items include the topic of Cricket.

Cricket runs through Keith Dunstan's writing from the 1930s Bodyline crisis he remembered from a Toorak childhood to the Centenary Test replay at the MCG where Premier Henry Bolte took guard in a spotted shirt with an L-plate pinned to his back.

Published 17 Aug 1968

A frustrated argument for reform of Test cricket between Australia and England, after a series of three draws in 1968, with former captains Ian Johnson and Lindsay Hassett diagnosing the slow over rate as the root cause of unfinished matches.
