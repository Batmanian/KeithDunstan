Topic: Bob Dalziel

6 items include the topic of Bob Dalziel.

Robert Paton Dalziel was Keith Dunstan's closest friend from Geelong Grammar and wartime RAAF training, an unpredictable, publicity-shy Melbourne stockbroker who made his fortune, retired at forty, then ran the R. E. Ross Foundation and Hillview Quarries until cancer killed him in 1978.
