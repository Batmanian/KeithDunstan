Topic: Victorian Railways

10 items include the topic of Victorian Railways.

The Victorian Railways was the government-owned rail network whose chronic derailment record and politically compromised freight pricing Keith Dunstan dissected in October 1964, noting that a British expert's 1949 diagnosis of muddled economy and a semi-bankrupt transport system remained accurate.

“A Century Could Be Just Around the Bend”
