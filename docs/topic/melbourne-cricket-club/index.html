Topic: Melbourne Cricket Club

6 items include the topic of Melbourne Cricket Club.

The Melbourne Cricket Club, custodian of the Melbourne Cricket Ground since 1853, was the subject of Keith Dunstan's first hardback book, 'The Paddock That Grew', published in 1962 to the giddy pride of a journalist who had never before seen his name on a spine.

Published 1962

Sir Edmund Herring's foreword to 'The Paddock That Grew', recalling the M.C.G.'s landmark cricket moments from W. G. Grace and the first Test match of 1877 through to his own boyhood memory of Victor Trumper making a pair in 1908.
