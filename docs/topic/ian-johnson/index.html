Topic: Ian Johnson

10 items include the topic of Ian Johnson.

Ian Johnson was a former Australian Test cricket captain who became secretary of the Melbourne Cricket Club, announcing plans just 48 hours before the 1963 VFL Grand Final to rebuild the Grey Smith Stand as the first stage of lifting the MCG's capacity to 150,000.
