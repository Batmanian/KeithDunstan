Topic: Melbourne Cricket Ground

29 items include the topic of Melbourne Cricket Ground.

The Melbourne Cricket Ground, or MCG, is Australia's largest sporting stadium and the shared home of cricket and Australian Rules football, a ground Keith Dunstan wrote the official history of and also remembered, less reverently, as the US-occupied wartime barracks where he was posted during the war.
