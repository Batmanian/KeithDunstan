Topic: Darwin

4 items include the topic of Darwin.

Darwin is the Northern Territory's tropical frontier capital, a town of barely a thousand people when novelist Xavier Herbert worked there as a young pharmacist and later Superintendent of Aborigines, and later a stop on Prince Philip's 1956 royal tour, remembered for its notorious late-night crocodile hunt.
