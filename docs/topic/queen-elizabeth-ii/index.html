Topic: Queen Elizabeth II

3 items include the topic of Queen Elizabeth II.

Queen Elizabeth II toured Australia several times during Keith Dunstan's career, most memorably on her marathon 1954 visit that saw 500 people collapse waiting for a glimpse of her in Melbourne, tours he covered as a young reporter and later wrote about at length in 'Supporting a Column'.

“Rediscovering the Yarra”

Published 01 Dec 1962

Previews the Queen's 1963 royal barge voyage up the Yarra to Henley Landing, and the belated riverbank clean-up it has prompted after years of floating trash and half-sunken barges.
