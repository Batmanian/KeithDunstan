Topic: Arthur Rylah

19 items include the topic of Arthur Rylah.

Arthur Rylah was Victoria's Chief Secretary and the state's chief censor through the 1960s, the official whose banning of books like Mary McCarthy's 'The Group' Keith Dunstan mocked by cataloguing the far racier pulp fiction still freely on sale.
