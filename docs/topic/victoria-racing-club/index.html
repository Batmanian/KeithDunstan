Topic: Victoria Racing Club

8 items include the topic of Victoria Racing Club.

The Victoria Racing Club runs Flemington Racecourse and the Melbourne Cup carnival, and in 1962, worried by falling attendances, launched the £7000 best-dressed-racegoer competition that Keith Dunstan covered as turning the horses into something close to an afterthought.
