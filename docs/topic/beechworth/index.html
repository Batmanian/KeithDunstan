Topic: Beechworth

6 items include the topic of Beechworth.

Beechworth is the historic gold-rush town in north-east Victoria where Ned Kelly was sentenced several times, and whose Burke Museum and Court House became focal points for a 1967 Kelly school convened by the Wangaratta Adult Education Centre.
