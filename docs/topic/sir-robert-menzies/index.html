Topic: Sir Robert Menzies

19 items include the topic of Sir Robert Menzies.

Sir Robert Menzies was Australia's longest-serving Prime Minister, leading the country continuously from 1949 to 1966 and appearing throughout Keith Dunstan's writing both as a recurring figure in his Bulletin columns on Melbourne's civic self-regard and, in his more ceremonial guise once knighted, bricking a bottle of Chateau Tahbilk red into a cellar wall not to be opened until 2060 and entertaining royalty on their tours of Australia.
