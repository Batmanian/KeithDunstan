Topic: Murray Rose

3 items include the topic of Murray Rose.

Murray Rose was the Australian Olympic swimmer and world record holder whose vegetarian diet disproved the old Australian belief that champion athletes had to be built on steak, and whose American celebrity Keith Dunstan cited as proof that Yankee resentment over Phar Lap's death had long since faded.
