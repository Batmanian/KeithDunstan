Topic: Sir George Pearce

1 item includes the topic of Sir George Pearce.

Sir George Pearce was the Western Australian-born federal politician and Defence Minister, nicknamed 'Minister for Massacres' for his handling of wartime conscription, whose views on wildlife and rural land management Keith Dunstan noted in 'Sports' as emblematic of official attitudes to the natural environment in the early twentieth century.

“The Killing Passion”

Published 1973

A history of Australia's enthusiasm for blood sports and wildlife slaughter, from the colonial shooting of Aborigines and lyrebirds through the kangaroo battues of the 1860s, the greyhound coursing craze of the 1870s and 1880s, the Great Emu War of 1932, the koala massacres of the 1920s, and the industrial-scale kangaroo kill of the postwar decades.
