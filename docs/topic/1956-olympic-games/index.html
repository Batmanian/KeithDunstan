Topic: 1956 Olympic Games

2 items include the topic of 1956 Olympic Games.

The 1956 Melbourne Olympics were the first Games held in the Southern Hemisphere, nicknamed the 'Friendly Games', and opened by Prince Philip after a tour of Papua and New Guinea that Keith Dunstan covered as a young reporter.

“Columns on HM and HRH”

Published 1966

Recalls the mass hysteria of the Queen's marathon 1954 tour, 500 collapsing from a day-long wait in Melbourne, and Prince Philip's 1956 tour of Papua and New Guinea, greeted by King Metakaka and his thirteen wives.

“The Athletic Passion”

Published 1973

A history of professional pedestrianism and athletics in Australia, from the colonial 'peds' and the great Melbourne-versus-Ireland sprint contests of the 1860s through the careers of Bobby McDonald, Charlie Samuels, Jack Donaldson, Arthur Postle and 'Professor' William Miller, to the Olympic triumphs of Marjorie Jackson, Betty Cuthbert, Herb Elliott and the heartbreaking near-misses of Ron Clarke.
