Topic: The Herald

7 items include the topic of The Herald.

The Herald was Melbourne's evening broadsheet and flagship of the Herald and Weekly Times group, the paper Keith Dunstan joined as a cadet reporter in the late 1940s despite his own father's warning that journalism led to a hack's life.

“The Capitol and ‘The World's Mightiest Spectacle.’”

Published 25 Jan 1964

On the closure of Walter Burley Griffin's Capitol Theatre, recalling its 1924 opening with "The Ten Commandments" and its Egyptian-god stage prelude, £15,000 Wurlitzer organ, and famous prismatic ceiling.

“When Melbourne Has 4.6 Million People . . .”

Published 18 Apr 1964

Gleefully projects Melbourne's population overtaking Sydney's by 1985 using National Cash Register's computer forecasts, then tours the just-finished National Mutual Centre, briefly Australia's biggest building, as the latest shot in a decades-long Melbourne-Sydney skyscraper rivalry that began with the ICI Building and the Conzinc Riotinto tower.

“The Athletic Passion”

Published 1973

A history of professional pedestrianism and athletics in Australia, from the colonial 'peds' and the great Melbourne-versus-Ireland sprint contests of the 1860s through the careers of Bobby McDonald, Charlie Samuels, Jack Donaldson, Arthur Postle and 'Professor' William Miller, to the Olympic triumphs of Marjorie Jackson, Betty Cuthbert, Herb Elliott and the heartbreaking near-misses of Ron Clarke.
