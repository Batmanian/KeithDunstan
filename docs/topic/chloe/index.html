Topic: Chloe

7 items include the topic of Chloe.

Chloe is the nude oil painting that caused a decorum scandal when first shown at Melbourne's National Gallery in 1883, was withdrawn within a month, and has hung ever since in the public bar of Young and Jackson's Hotel, becoming, as Keith Dunstan noted, one of the city's biggest tourist draws.
