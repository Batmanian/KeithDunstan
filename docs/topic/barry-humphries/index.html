Topic: Barry Humphries

12 items include the topic of Barry Humphries.

Barry Humphries (1934-2023) was an Australian comedian and satirist best known for creating the character Dame Edna Everage, along with figures like the boorish cultural attaché Sir Les Patterson, building a decades-long career sending up suburban Australian manners.

“To the Spencer Street Station”

Published 21 Apr 1962

Compares the muted arrival of the new Southern Aurora express at Spencer Street with the wild 1883 celebrations when Melbourne and Sydney's rail lines first met at Albury, then covers Victoria's new ban on tattooing minors.
