Topic: 6 Oclock swill

4 items include the topic of 6 Oclock swill.

The six o'clock swill was the mad rush to down as many beers as possible before hotel bars shut at 6pm, a wartime austerity measure that lingered in Victoria until 1966 and which Keith Dunstan remembered as a defining, faintly absurd feature of 1950s Melbourne drinking.
