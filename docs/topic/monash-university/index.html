Topic: Monash University

12 items include the topic of Monash University.

Monash University is Melbourne's second university, founded in 1958 at Clayton, home to historian Ian Turner who delivered the annual Ron Barassi Memorial Lectures on the sociology of Australian Rules football, which Keith Dunstan cited in 'Sports' for Turner's analysis of football as a substitute for warfare.

“The Monash Story: Keeping Them Down on the Farm”

Published 02 May 1964

A portrait of Monash University three years after its unexpectedly early opening, examining its car-dependent Clayton campus, its race to become the world's fastest-growing university, its chronic underfunding, and the outsized personalities shaping its character.
