Topic: Flemington Racecourse

17 items include the topic of Flemington Racecourse.

Flemington Racecourse is home to the Melbourne Cup and the Victoria Racing Club, and the venue Keith Dunstan reported from during Queen Elizabeth II's 1963 visit, when the VRC banned television cameras only to see attendance fall well short of the Queen Mother's earlier, televised meeting.
