The bicycle boom

2 min read.

First published 25 Oct 1975

Melbourne is experiencing an unprecedented bicycle boom, with Malvern Star releasing a limited-edition Sovereign Star at $560 and European racers like Peugeot and Raleigh selling out, though the lack of bicycle paths in Melbourne remains a serious impediment to the expansion of cycling as a practical means of transport.

THE bicycle boom continues. Bicycles are selling in numbers that the industry has never seen before. The bicycle shops cannot keep up with demand. Good second-hand bicycles are fetching prices that would have seemed absurd five years ago.

Malvern Star has launched a special limited edition bicycle, the Sovereign Star. It costs $560. This is a remarkable price for a bicycle — more than many people earn in a week. The Sovereign Star is beautifully made, beautifully finished. It sold out immediately.

The European racing bicycles — Peugeot, Raleigh, Gitane — are also selling well. These are serious bicycles, designed for serious cyclists. They are light, fast, responsive. People who buy them use them. Melbourne sees more cyclists than it has ever seen.

But Melbourne is not built for cyclists. The roads are wide, fast, dangerous. There are almost no bicycle paths. The cyclist on a Melbourne road is always in danger. Cars pass too close. Trucks are terrifying. Intersections are hazardous.

The city needs bicycle paths urgently. London has been building them. Amsterdam has had them for generations. Tokyo has them. Melbourne has almost none.

The argument against bicycle paths is always cost. The road space that would be given to bicycles costs money to build and maintain. This is true. But it is also true that every person on a bicycle is not in a car. If Melbourne could persuade a significant percentage of its commuters to use bicycles, the reduction in traffic congestion and pollution would be substantial.

Source: Batman's Melbourne: The bicycle boom, The Bulletin, 25 October 1975. Accessible via the National Library of Australia's Trove database.
