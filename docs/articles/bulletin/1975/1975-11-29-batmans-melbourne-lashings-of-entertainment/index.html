Lashings of entertainment

2 min read.

First published 29 Nov 1975

At Old Sydney Town near Gosford, Keith Dunstan watches convict re-enactments including a public flogging of Jane Jones twice daily, performed in a half-built St Phillip's Church by actors including Eric Thiison in the role of a convict, while the 80-foot brig Perseverance serves as the centrepiece of the colonial settlement.

OLD Sydney Town is near Gosford in New South Wales. It is a recreation of colonial Sydney in the early days — the convict period, the military settlement, the beginning of the colony.

The floggings are performed twice daily. I was just in time for the 11.30 am session. The temporary court was in the half-built St Phillip's Church. The redcoats marched in with Jane Jones, a rather bedraggled figure. In thundering tones a corporal read out the charge. She had stolen silver and linen from Elizabeth Rafferty.

'It's all a pack of lies,' wailed Jane. 'The miserable old bitch planted the stuff under my bed.'

The crowd loved it. The sentence was delivered. Jane was taken away. The whole performance was done with great conviction by actors who clearly enjoyed their work.

Eric Thiison plays the convict. He is the centrepiece of Old Sydney Town's historical program. He knows the period extraordinarily well and he brings it alive with remarkable skill.

The 80-foot brig Perseverance is the centrepiece of the harbour. It sits in the water at Old Sydney Town and it is a magnificent vessel — beautifully restored, properly rigged, completely convincing. You can go aboard and walk the deck and get some sense of what it was like to arrive in this country as a convict, chained below, knowing you would not see England again.

The colony did terrible things to people. Old Sydney Town does not flinch from this. The floggings were real. The conditions were brutal. The museum does not clean this up.

Source: Batman's Melbourne: Lashings of entertainment, The Bulletin, 29 November 1975. Accessible via the National Library of Australia's Trove database.
