Articles by publication

The Sun

Keith wrote for The Herald and Weekly Times from 1946 to 1978, most notably in 'The Sun News-Pictorial's 'A Place in the Sun' from 1958 to 1978.

Walkabout Magazine

Keith wrote for Walkabout Magazine between 1962 and 1969

Top topics: Melbourne, Surfers Paradise, Cape York Peninsula, Harold Holt, Sun News-Pictorial, Toowoomba, 6 Oclock swill, Aborigines, Adelaide Festival of Arts, Alice Springs, All Saints Winery, Antarctica, Anti-Football League, Archer (racehorse), Arthur Calwell

The Bulletin

Keith wrote in The Bulletin from 1962 until 1984, initially under the psuedonym, 'Batman'.

Top topics: Melbourne, Toorak, Henry Bolte, St Kilda, Carlton and United Breweries, Maurice Nathan, Moomba, Southern Cross Hotel, Sydney, Victoria Racing Club, Bourke Street, Collins Street, Essendon, Exhibition Building, Harold Holt

The Age

Keith wrote for The Age occasionally from 1986 until his death. Collections of his columns for The Age can be found in his books "No Brains on Tuesday" and "A Day in the Life of Australia".
