No Brains on Tuesday

Published in 1991, 'No Brains on Tuesday' collects the columns Keith Dunstan wrote for the Age and Sydney Morning Herald after 34 years at the Sun News-Pictorial, a small sequel to his 1990 autobiography 'No Brains At All'.

Foreword

Michael Smith, editor of the Age, introduces the collection: 'None of this is to under-estimate the power of Dunstan's journalism. He has been Melbourne's best-read columnist for more than 30 years.'
