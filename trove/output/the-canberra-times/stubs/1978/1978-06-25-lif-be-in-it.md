Life. Be in it Have you ever thought of seriously taking up cycling?' Hei-sare some of the advantages and disadvantages listed by Keith Dunstan. Advantages Fun: It really is a joyful experience, riding a bike. You ^ see your town. Get the sights, smells and feel of your surroundings that you never get by car. Healthy: It cures aches and pains. I had persistent hip rheumatic pains, not any more. Freedom: It will go anywhere except on water. Cheap: Sell your car, ride a bike and save $3,000 a year. Disadvantages Terror: Frequently motorists try to frighten you. Pollution: Cars. You have to smell them. Personal pollution: Summertime, Girls sit a little further away. Social consequences: Friends tell you daily that you are out of your mind. At times you even suffer a sense of inferiority. (From 'Habitat Australia', April, 1977) Save yourself some aches and pains. Before skiing for the first time this season, do some ski exercises. The ACT Ski Council is running classes at the Holder High School Hall on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. Phone Jim Duncan on 881622 AH for more information. If the sun is shining take the kids for a stroll after "brunch" or early in the afternoon. Find a pine forest and gather pine cones for your fire place or for the kids to paint. If it's raining (we're getting used to that on Sundays) find your old train set or slot cars and build a track oib I the living-room floor.
