From KEITH DUNSTAN and AAP MANCHESTER, Thur. — It rained last night, it rained this morning, it rained dur ing the afternoon and it rained again this evening: In between, England and Australia had 168 minutes that weren't too wet or dull for cricket. And if the rain and the uncertainty of the weather weren't sufficient to oppress the spirits of even the most hardy players, the misfortunes of the day must surely have done so . . .
