Where tha' blazes d'yer think yer going?" Plrbright is 28 miles from Lon don and is being used as a train ing camp for a stiffly disciplined Guards' battalion. Salutes flick up like railway signals. The Guards are now getting ready for an influx of Austra lians, Canadians, South Africans, Gurkhas and all visiting contin gents for the Coronation. The Guards are a little dismay ed about how they will cope with the odd mixture.
