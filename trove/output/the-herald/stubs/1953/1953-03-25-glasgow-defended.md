? Glasgow defended KEITH DUNSTAN (Herald, 21/3/53) gives the impression that Glasgow Is a place where very little matters except ships and dirt. I live at Heathmont. but I wouldn't write home and tell my friends in Glasgow that I live 13 miles from Melbourne, without roads or sewerage, and that I use oil lamps. Don't think I don't like Austra lia, but I have found more dirt, dust and mud here in months than in 20 years' experience throughout the entire Clyde. — R. S. GILLESPIE. Erica Crescent. Heathmont. 4r
