Frank to prove his love flew- half-way round the world to see her. They met and kissed at a Spanish air port (so the columnists said). Then into the news came Pepita Marco, Mario's fiancee, a beautiful black-eyed, blaek- haired Spanish dancer. While In New York she read of the doings in Spain, and the New York Daily News took photo graphs of her furiously tearing up Mario's love letters. For weeks on end nobody knew who loved who. On the strength of this the Artists League of America voted Ava "the world's most provocative female,"' and a Paris newspaper published a photograph of her in bathing costume above the single cap tion, "Delicieuse." From that time on Frankie and Ava were the chatter- of the columnists. The publicity came in droves, and the gossip writers considered it worthy of note that she hated nighties, and always preferred pyjamas, that she hated breakfast in bed, and got up early to de velop an appetite. One writer reported that she was a simple girl at heart, that she loathed make-up, and that a Los Angeles bank had mis taken her for a school girl when she walked in wearing jeans, sandals, and no lipstick. Miss Gardner was also re ported as being fed-up at Frank Sinatra's tardiness in getting a divorce. But now his wife Nancy has announced she will file suit for divorce in several weeks. In 1945 she married Artie Shaw. Artie, much to her disgust, in sisted that she .improve her mind with Marcel Proust. Plato and Thomas Mann. This ' marriage also lasted a year.
