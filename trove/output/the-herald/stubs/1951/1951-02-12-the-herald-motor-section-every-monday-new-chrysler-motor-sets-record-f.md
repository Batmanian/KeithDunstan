New Chrysler Motor Sets Record For Power From KEITH DUNSTAN NEW YORK, Sunday. — Chrysler's Im perial and New Yorker models for 1951 have the most powerful engine yet built for private cars. It develops 180 brake horsepower, compared with 135 in previous Chryslers, but it is lighter and more compact than its predecessors. The new engine is a secret on which the Chrysler Cor poration has been working for nearly five years. The great power comes from rounded combustion chambers coupled with a lateral over head valve arrangement. This design is employed in aircraft engines and racing cars. New Chryslers also have an arrangement called "hydra- guide power steering." It Is claimed that it cuts out nine- tenths of the manual effort in steering. A hydraulic mechanism at tached to the steering makes it possible to drive with just a thumb and one finger on the wheel. Buick Change All the 1951 American cars have bigger bumpers, more elaborate dashboards and big ger windows. But, apart from this. It is difficult to tell them from 1950 models. Buick has redesigned its front end so that thebumper is once again in front of the grill. The Roadmaster has new- type brakes similar to those used in tanks. The lining is divided into segments cemented to the drum. According to com pany reports, it assures more positive action and longer wear. Now almost every make, from Ford to Cadillac, offers the choice of conventional drive or automatic transmis sion. The automobile industry has ushered in Its 1951 models with none of the usual lavish cock tail parties, beautiful girls and shows at the Waldorf Astoria. Defence Work Instead, all the big com panies, such as Ford, General Motors and Chrysler, are tool ing up to cater for huge orders for tanks, trucks, engines and aircraft. Metals are scarce, and cars produced later this year will have less chrome. Motor writers here forecast that exports of cars will drop at least 50 per cent, this year. On the home market, supplies will also drop 40 to 50 per cent.
