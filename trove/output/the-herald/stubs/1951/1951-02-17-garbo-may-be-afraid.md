GARBO MAY BE AFRAID Keith Dunstan's Hollywood NEW YORK. — Hollywood 'is trying desperately hard to bring Greta Garbo back to the screen.' Every week she receives new offers, but still the strange Miss Garbo remains aloof, mysterious. Of all the strange legends that have come from Hollywood during the past 20 years, her legend is the strangest. She has not made a picture for 10 years, yet her fame is still as strong as ever. Young, people who have never seen her are dying to And out the source of her magic. Older people want her back to catch up with a memory. Critics here still believe she is me greatest actress ever to appear ori the American screen and one of the most beautiful women of this century. Ever since she retired rumors have run wild that she would make a come-back this month, next month or certainly next year. Sometimes production was all ready to start but the projects seemed to fizzle and die. But this time many people in the industry believe she really will come back. First Gloria Swanson has ' proved and proved very capably that it can be done. Two, Miss Garbo has just become an American citizen. Three, she has put herself in the hands of the Charles Feldman Agency, an agency which handles business for many of Hollywood's highest paid stars. According to some reports she is already reading through scripts. When writing about Greta Garbo it is diflicult to discover where the legend begins and ends. There is no evidence she ever did say "I want to be al-o-o-ne" and "I tank I go home." Yet it is true she never entered into the night life of Hollywood. She loathed night clubs and she refused to go near well-known restaurants. She was a vegetarian and lived mostly on vegetable juices. ? THE real mystery is why she has re fused for 10 years to make a picture. During her career she made 25 films and according to the critics all of them extremely good. Now she has a terror of being photographed, a fear of being seen and constantly she tries to keep away from people. This apparently is no act or publicity stunt. She does seem to be genuinely afraid of people. She appears to have a highly developed neurosis which amounts to a feeling of inferiority — a fear that another film would shatter her incredible reputation for beauty and superlative acting. Slie has reacted to this in the only way she knows how — she has shut herself off from the world and everything to do with it. She is now 45. The whole world and particularly her native land Sweden want her to come back. She once said of Hollywood; "I tried to be Swedish, but it's difficult to try anything. There is no time for art. All that matters is what they call box office." Quite obviously her decision is not an easy one. If Holly wood wants her back, it must handle her. with very consider able tact. ? TALKING of come backs, Harold Lloyd has just made his first film in -14 years. He appears in a crazy farce called "Mad Wednesday." He is still the same wise-crack ing Mr Lloyd with the same heavy horn-rimmed glasses. Fred Astaire and Ginger Rogers may also make another film. "The Barkleys of Broad way" was such a success that it was one of the five top money makers for 1948. Jerry Wald and Norman Krasna want to co-star the team in a I picture which would be the story of their career togethe'r. Wald and Krasna have put together some clippings of the best numbers they did in their heyday. These sequences have not dated and to remake them now would cost millions — more than any studio could afford. Miss Rogers commented: "Of all our screen dancers I think Fred's tops in style, class and inventiveness. Of course, if audiences want us teamed in more pictures. I'd certainly be happy to oblige." '
