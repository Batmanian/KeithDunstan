Even "Red Shoes," which ran for two years, two months, didn't open as well as this. Perhaps what startled .people here so much was the way Emeric Pressburger and Michael Powell, its producers, spent so much money and talent on this film. After all, Americans expect the British to be conservative and never, never to attempt either the gigantic or the colossal. But "Tales of Hoffmann," a fantastic opera which took Offenbach 30 years- to write, takes up two hours 20 minutes of film, about the same time that It does on the stage.
