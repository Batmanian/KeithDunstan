Mr Malik Orders The Gold Service At The Waldorf From KEITH DUNSTAN NEW YORK, Friday. — The dinner Russia's Mr Malik gave for members of the Security Council at the Waldorf Astoria last night was In the very best Capitalist tradition. Mr Malik himself looked extremely well and ate very well for a man who refused to meet U.N. officials for five days because of ill health. The dinner cost £15 a head. The flowers alone cost the Soviet £45. One delegate said there was virtually no talk about peace moves in Korea. Instead the guests chatted mostly about vodka, Russian champagne and Russian caviar. The meal began with cold smoked turkey, salmon and sturgeon served on silver salvers. Next came stuffed avocado with crab meat sauce and imported Russian caviar with chopped egg and onion. The main course was Supreme de Plntade a la Perogordina — in other words pheasant — with Crimean champagne. The dessert was a souffle with fresh strawberries served very cold at Mr Malik's express order. Finally there were red mints, fruit, cog- nac. and corona coronas served on the Waldorf's gold service, also at Mr Malik's express order. Two men were not there — Nationalist China's Mr Tingfu tsiang and the U.N. Sec- retary-General, Mr Trygve Lie. They were not invited.
