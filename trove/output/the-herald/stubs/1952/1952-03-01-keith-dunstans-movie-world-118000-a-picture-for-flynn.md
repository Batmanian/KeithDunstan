Keith Dunstan's | Movie World £118,000 a picture for Flynn New york. — Errol Flynn, the Tasmanian-born film star, has won Hollywood's most fantastic contract. For Warner Brothers he will now make just one picture a year, and for that, they will pay him £118,000. Furthermore, this one I picture must not take up more than 14 weeks of his time. The rest of the year he can make as many pic tures as h wishes for the other studios.
