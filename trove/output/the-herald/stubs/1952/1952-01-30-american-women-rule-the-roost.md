American women rule the roost From Keith Dunstan NEW YORK. — The American female, always an indomitable, inde pendent creature, is gaining control of this, the wealthiest, the most powerful nation on earth. And if you are one of those people who believe that: money is everything, she has won the battle hands down. . . THE poor male who fought so nobly against the suffragettes has lost his power, his prestige and even his right, to at least bring home the pay envelope. The sad news- has just been pub lished. In New York. Women spend 85 cents out of every dollar. This leaves the men a mere 15 cents as Eocket -money. They actually own etween 60 and 70 per cent, of the nation's wealth and they consti tute 43 per cent, of the stockhold ers who.own the corporations of America. Then you find out that the United States Treasurer is a woman. Her signature, Georgia Neese Clarke, appears on every dollar bill. And if you think she -is just a figurehead with some bril liant male behind her, the Assist ant Treasurer is also a woman, Mrs Mabelle Kennedy. Imagine a woman who is capable of signing a receipt for 27,424,541,- 375 dollars and 78 cents. Mrs Clarke . did this the other, day for gold, silver and Government securities locked away in Washington's vaults; A woman runs the U.S. mint Just to round off the picture, the direptor of the Mint is but an other woman — Mrs Nellie Ross. Women are a greater power in this country than most men real ise. They make up more than one- thjrd of the labor force' of 60 mil lion and more than ' half these women workers are married. How they work, raise their fami- lies and look after their husbands at the same time would be a mys tery to women In Australia. They show a determination to get out of the house, away .from the boredom and loneliness of housework, which is remarkable. They buy all the gadgets to cut down on housework and whenever possible they take the. family out to the local restaurant; They leave. their children all day at kin dergartens. Or if the children are too young for this, mother often spends every cent she earns on baby sitters. Without the huge female labor force the nation's factories, Gov ernment departments and even the defence forces would be crippled. ; The banks are a good example of this. More than 60 per cent, of all the bank jobs and 10 per cent. of the bank executive positions are held by women. Among the execu tives of America's banks are 19 board chairmen, 117 presidents arid 300 vice-presidents — all women. Fortunately women have yet to control the Senate and there has-' never been a female President. But every year women gain a few more- of the top Government posts. She drafts men into the army Anna Rosenberg, as Assistant Secretary of Defence, is one of the most powerful figures in the U.S. She has played a major part in organising the country for war and it is her job to draft millions of- men into the Army. She pushes around more men than Alexander the Great ever did. Female' diplomats are becoming popular. Ferle Mesta is the Minis ter to Luxembourg. Mrs Eugenie Anderson is the. U.S. Ambassador to Denmark. Mrs Vera Davis is president ' of the Delaware State- Senate, and In 1949 Mrs Dorothy Lee was elected Mayor of Port land, Oregon. Mrs Frieda Hennock, much to the horror of male law- yers, is a judge of the Federal Dis trict Court of New York. It is difficult to think of a field in which' the women don't try to outdo the . men. Women wrestlers and boxers are quite a hit on- tele vision. ' A. young Texan, Patricia McCormack, 22, has speared her first bulls in the bull rings of Mexico. - - According to the statistics \yomen have a lower death rate as babies and they live seven years longer than the average male. It's all rather humiliating. And to/makel the humiliation worse the American Automobile Association claims that women are better drivers than men. The AAA said men have- 2.8 accidents a mil- , lion miles, compared with 1.7 for women. They drive more slowly, more carefully and they give more hand signals. We men have something to think about; But seeing the women have all this ability, all this bril liance in financial matters, all this enthusiasm for work, perhaps the time has come for them to take over. ' We could then settle down to the important things, like the arts,- . tennis, golf, sun bathing and med dling in the garden. MRS CLARKE no figurehead . . . MRS MESTA in Luxembourg ...
