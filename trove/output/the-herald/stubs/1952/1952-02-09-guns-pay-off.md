Keith Dunstan's Film World Guns pay off NEW YORK. — If it had happened just once it might have been a fluke, but now for the second year running the United States has voted John Wayne as its most popular film star. Isn't that curious? WHEN you ask people to an- alyse Mr Wayne's success they look at you blankly. It is like asking them why do people like hot dogs or why do Americans drive on the right-hand side of, the road. ' But there's no doubt about his success. Re cently he was starring at nine Los Angeles theatres at once and every -time you turn on a television set, there he is. galloping the prairies, no hands, with both ,44s blazing. Yet he has -had no big publicity build-ups, the big magazines rarely run articles about him, and his name hardly ever makes the newspapers. As a personality he's not even half as exciting as Donald Duck. In fact it's hard to write an article about him. He has none of the usual Hollywood eccen tricities. He is so popular, maybe because he is everybody's idea of a cowboy — tall, lean, tough, no spare flesh and no fancy business. His body is all curves and ac tion, his arms hanging loose at his sides, his hands never more than three inches from his pistol butts. He made 75 cowboy features and serials in a row. He told one film writer: "I think I've had more bad pictures than anyone who has survived in Hollywood. "In Western quickies you not only try to act and keep your dialogue from sounding as phony as it is, but you take a physical beating in them— and you do it all in 10 days or so. "Then maybe you get half a week's lay-off ana you're back in another with the story always the same — hero - heroine - horse - mortgage - chase - fight - He said that in those days he spent weeks study ing the style of the lead ing rodeo performer and cowboy Yakima Canutt. He learned to walk smooth ly, slowly, rolling his hips, so as to give tne impres sion of quiet strength." He learnt to talk , softly without any emotion' "yet at the same time with an even latent power." He was a steely character in deed. His size made him omin ous enough but the studio emphasised this by making all the doors smaller, so that he had to stoop to enter a room. Then they wiped out his peaches ana cream complexion with heavy dark make-up. WAYNE
