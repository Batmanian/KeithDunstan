The film is now showing at New York's Roxy Theatre, which has 6000 seats, and claims the title of the world's second- largest movie house. "Kangaroo," the first Hollywood film made on location In Australia, was an expensive item, so the publicity machines are rolling at full steam. The Australian ballad singer "Smokey" Dawson has arrived here with a large kangaroo named "Zip." "Smokey" intended to show off "Zip" at the Roxy, along with a little singing of "Waltzing Matilda," but "Zip" escaped from Smokey's" car out on Long Island, and easily dodged all the search par ties that went after him.
