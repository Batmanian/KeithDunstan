Black Market City rJ’HERE is a good time to be had in Hong Kong, and good things to buy — if you have -plenty of money. Prices are at a fantastic level and the city is just one big black market with the police closing their eyes to it. This is one aspect of Hong Kong’s hectic post-war scene, to be described by Keith Dunstan in the magazine pages of The Herald today.
