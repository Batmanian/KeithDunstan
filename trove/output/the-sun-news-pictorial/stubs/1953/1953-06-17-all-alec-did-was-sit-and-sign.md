All Alec did was sit — and sign From Keith Duns tan I NOTTINGHAM, Tues.—Alex Bed ser was sitting by himself in the dress ing-room bitterly dis appointed. All lie had to do was autograph the pile of scorecards which 'car ried his incredible bowl ing figures. "It’s bad luck, rare bad luck.” he said. “But it's all in the game. “I can’t say more or I’d have my head knocked off. and they wouldn’t put me in the next Test." Bedser. big. modest and amiable, has taken his triumph quietly. After the big day Saturday there were no celebrations. He simply went to the pictures. The story goes that lie met Hassett at a hotel. The tiny Hassett looked at him and said: “I ought to kick you in the head.” Replied Pig Alec: “Okay. Will I get you a step-ladder?”
