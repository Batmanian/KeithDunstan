'Heard in Passing' 004-61 1258 by Pat Dolman I saw in the Age 'Melbourne Living' that Keith Dunstan is to have a regular column each Tuesday - DUNSTAN. Keith Dunstan has been a well known journalist and author for many years and his first column was an entertaining but useful look at the role of women in the church, mainly in this case, the Anglican Communion. He commented that 90% of the work is done by women and even that more formidable group, the Mother's Union, has never learnt to organise itself as a union. Keith Dunstan asked what would happen if a Norma Gallagher or a Joanna Halfpenny took command - he thought the end result would soon be a break through of the anti-female barrier! He probably had his tongue in his cheek but with the rise of women power, he feels that the Anglican Church could only prosper with the ordination of women and if anyone was in doubt about who was the male or female priest, it would be the one with the short hair (and I might add, the one without the beard, which is almost a 'must' with the younger clergy).
