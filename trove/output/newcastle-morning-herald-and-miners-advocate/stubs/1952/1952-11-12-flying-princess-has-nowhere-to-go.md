Saunders Roe originally de signed the 'Princess for the Atlantic run, and. she was to fly direct from London to New York. She was to have everything the Queen Mary had, bar the swimrmng pool luxury :sleeping berths, cock tail bars and smoking rooms. British Overseas Airways Corporation abandoned the Princess for two reasons. They wanted to go all out for the Comets, and they felt the cost of running flying-boat bases, lust for .the Princess alone, would be too heavy. This is a real tragedy for Saunders Roe. Sir Arthur Goudge, the same man who gave us the Empire flying-boat, also de signed the Princess. She is a beautiful piece of engineering. There are 10 Proteus turbo prop engines which deliver a total of 35,000 horsepower, Eight of them are mounted in pairs, and they drive con tra-rotating propellers. This is an almost unbeliev able concentration of power -enough to drive the huge bulk through the air at 380 n.p.h. for 3500 miles. Enough to take 200 migrants to. They will go into mothballs, to wait in storage till someone' thinks of the Princess boats, worth more than £2 million each, as some thing more than an interesting experiment.
