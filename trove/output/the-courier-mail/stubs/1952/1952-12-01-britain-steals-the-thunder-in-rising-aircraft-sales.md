Rival best-seller to the Dove is the Bristol Freighter, a lumbering, great creature, about as graceful as a whale. British confident The competition with Ame rica will be something hectic, as there are fortunes to be made, and more than £1000 million to be shared out. The British manufacturers are incredibly confident about this battle. They are sure they are ahead and will stay ahead. It is a battle they can't af ford to lose.
