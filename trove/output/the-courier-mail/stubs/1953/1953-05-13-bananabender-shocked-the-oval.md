"BANANA-BENDER" SHOCKED THE OVAL From KEITH DUNSTAN THE OVAL, May 12—"Cripes 'ave a go, will yer,'" bawled a raucous barracker at the staid Oval to the slow-scoring Surrey batsmen. Then he bawled "Good on yer, Aussie" And when young Queens- land giant Ron Archer got his wickets, the barracker, obviously a gravel-voiced renegade from the Brisbane Cricket Ground, yelled: "Not bad for a banana-bender." In the members' stand they thought this was all very odd. One member said to an other: "We will have to put up a sign, " English spoken here." The gloom was something awful. Australia had routed Surrey in less than two days of a three-day match. To-morrow the Australians will begin another three-day match — this time against Cambridge University. Archer's claim — P 12.
