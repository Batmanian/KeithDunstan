COLUMNIST ANTI-SPORT “There Is too much space devoted to sport in the newspapers,” said “Sun” columnist Keith Dunstan when he ad* dressed the National Council of Jewish Women at a recent luncheon at the Hotel Australia. The press should pay more attention, Mr. Dun stan felt, to cultural aspects of life. _ A man of his convic tions, Mr. Dunstan is president of the Anti Football League. Originat ing from a humorous sug gestion in Dunstan’s daily column two years ago, this league has attracted a large following, especi ally from among the “football widows.” Mr. Dunstan showed the ladies the membership badge — a square foot ball, designed not to bounce. Ironically, the owner of the firm which makes these badges, Sir Kenneth Luke, is president • KEITH DUNSTAN of the VFL! However, its all in a good cause ... all money contributed to the Anti Football League is given to charity. Next function of NCJW will be an address on abortion, delivered by Dr. B. Wainer.
