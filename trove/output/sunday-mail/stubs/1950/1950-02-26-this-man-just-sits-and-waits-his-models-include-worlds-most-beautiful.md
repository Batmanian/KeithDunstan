THIS MAN JUST SITS AND WAITS His models include world's most beautiful women GLAMOUR IS A BUSINESS WITH JOHN R. POWERS From KEITH DUNSTAN in New York NEW YORK, Sat. — John Robert Powers, undisputed king of America's model world, leaned back in his chair, and said, rather smugly: 'All I have to do is sit and wait. Almost every attractive girl eventually finds her way to my office.' There was no point in making a smart reply, the man was so obviously right. Plastered around the walls of his office were dozens of pictures of the loveliest women America has ever seen. Among the best known of these one-time 'Powers girls,' but' not necessarily the most beautiful, are Dolores Costello, Barbara Stan wyck, Kay Francis, Paulette Goddard, Joan Blondell Jennifer Jones, Gene Tierney, and Marsha Hunt. I expected Mr Powers to be a smooth, dapper person, with a Ronald Colman moustache, but instead I found him a ruddy faced person, more like Spencer Tracy. During the last 20 years, Mr. Powers has interviewed more than 500,000 girls, sometimes at the rate of hundreds a day, but he has only Here is a typical sleek, well- groomed model in the American style. Her bouf- font white frock is set off by a wide cyclamen sash at the waist. asked one-half of one per cent. to wait around. When asked how he picked them, he said: 'A successful model must have more than just good looks. She must have poise, grace, good carriage, im agination, advertising instinct, and the ability to strike the correct pose for a photo grapher. 'A girl must learn to move in rhythm and to present herself gracefully. Too many girls, who could be beautiful, clomp about a room like spirited drayhorses. They plaster themselves so heavily with make-up that they have to scrape it off with a putty knife.' These qualifications of grace and poise are hard enough to come by, but there are others even more exacting. Fashion magazines, and exclusive stores, like to have models who fit ex actly into their fashion designs. Earn big wages THIS means, that the model, unless she has other super qualities, must be something of a freak to make the grade. Here are the required meas urements: — Height in heels 5ft. 9in.; bust, 34in.; waist, 24in.; hips, 34in. They may be freaks, but once they have won their way into the select circle of the 100 Powers' models, they have a chance of making big money. Most of them earn from £2 to £6 an hour, those who are better known £10 an hour. The star performers whose faces are known to every fashionable woman in America make anything up to £18 an hour. The headquarters for these models is on the sixth floor of a Park Avenue skyscraper. The centre of operations is a bank of 36 telephones with direct lines to the great fashion houses. At least six of these jangle constantly. Look alike IN the surrounding rooms one always sees half a dozen or so incredibly beautiful women. There is something the same about all of them. They are all tall, impec cably dressed with a kind of cold, aloof, untouchable beauty. Some have the aristocratic, fine type of features for which the artistocracy yearn, and they help to sell the exotic clothing which is sold at the rate of millions of dol lars a day on Fifth Avenue. They provide the happy illusion for the ordinary woman who thinks she will look as fine if she buys that same photographed gown for 1000 dollars (£A446). There is no mistaking a fashion model in New York. Apart from the way they wear a dress or do their hair, you can spot them because they always carry a hat box. It's a tradition which started 15 years ago, when a model asked Mr. Powers for something in which to carry her spare dresses and make up kit from assignment to as signment. Mr. Powers gave her a hat box. First agency THE hat box was like everything else, it just caught on. Mr. Powers, although he is now internationally famous, was iust a penniless actor 22 years ago. Even then, he had a knack for meeting and knowing people. Photographers were always ringing up and asking him where they could find a good looking girl for some fashion pictures. Mr. Powers gave them the telephone numbers of some of his hard-up act ress friends. Mrs. Powers, an attractive blonde, said:. 'Look, we have to eat, too. Why don't you start a business bringing these people together?' And so the first model agency was born.
