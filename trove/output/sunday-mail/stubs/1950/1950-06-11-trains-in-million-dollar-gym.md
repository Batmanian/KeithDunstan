TRAINS IN MIUION DOLLAR GYM. From KEITH DUNSTAN NEW YORK, Sat. — The 55-metre practice pool where Australian and world record breaker, John Marshall, trains at Yale.University, is* housed in one of the largest and most amazing gym nasiums in the world. ' - It is built on tne tines of bl cathedral, eight storeys bigh with rose windows and made of tapestry brick. Yale built it in the de pression days for £2,400, 000, but now, it must be worth at least £7,000,000. Besides the practice pool there is an exhibition pool with seating space for nearly 3000 people. The gymnasium has a boxing floor with six rings and sound proofed rooms for bag punching. There are rooms for fencing, volley ball, bad minton, and basket ball. It has' space for practis ing golf and polo as well as 30 squash and eight hand ball courts. Just so toe swimmers won't -have to dive into the pool feeling cold, there are dry and steam heat chambers. Then when they feel tired between races there is a dormitory which they can use for a little sleep. For rowers there are three rowing pools, which 'take crews of eight These are amazing- affairs, which almost exactly simulate actual rowing conditions. Pumps pass water through the pools at any speed to keep pace with the move ment of the oars. This land rowing saves all' the un pleasant business of rowing miles up and down rivers. At Yale they can row in air conditioned comfort. ? THERE are many rooms for various forms of gymnastics. There is a rifle range, too, and a basket ball amphi-theatre, which seats 3400 spectators. The stu dents also use the amphi theatre for playing tennis. John Marshall says that almost everybody hi the University, roughly 8000 sfudents, could play a sport of some kind there at the one time. Every student at Yale must be able to chin the bar eight times, do 25 push ups, 50 sit-ups, and also cer tain jumping tests. John, when he first arrived, could not do the bar chinning test, but he passed it after a little practice. Also every student when he enrols is told to strip. Then he is -taken to the pos ture room, where instruc tors photograph his body and decide what exercises be needs. After looking over this gymnasium it is easy to see why America leads the world in athletics.
