# Sources for the revised home page concepts

These are proposed editorial compositions, not approved site copy.

- Lawrence Money, [Keith Dunstan, Australian Media Hall of Fame](https://halloffame.melbournepressclub.com/article/keith-dunstan). Supports his long-running column, the inaugural Melbourne Press Club Lifetime Achievement Award, Anti-Football League involvement, and the columnist award named after him. The short description “acute, gentle and generous of spirit” is an excerpt of Barry Humphries’s tribute quoted by Money, not a newly sourced direct interview.
- Lawrence Money, [Melbourne Press Club Lifetime Achievement profile](https://www.melbournepressclub.com/uploads/Awards/LTAA/1995keithdunstan3.pdf). Supports his foreign correspondence, daily and weekly columns and founding presidency of the Bicycle Institute of Victoria in 1974.
- The Hall of Fame profile and the archive disagree on exact column duration; the Hall of Fame text itself uses different totals. Avoid numerical durations until reconciled. The Hall of Fame cycling date also conflicts with the award profile, so use the latter for the 1974 presidency.
- No new historical imagery is sourced for these concepts. The existing archive portrait is the image reference; generated mockup portraits must be replaced by the original file in implementation.
