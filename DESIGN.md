# Existing visual system

Recorded from the incumbent implementation for home page composition studies, not a replacement identity.

- White light-mode surface, dark charcoal text and Bootstrap success green (`#198754`) for the brand, links and accents.
- Inter with sans-serif fallbacks; restrained weight contrast and generous whitespace.
- Bootstrap container grid, straightforward navigation and simple bordered/accordion treatments.
- Documentary portrait and real book covers carry the subject. No fabricated archival photographs or newspaper scans.
- Dark mode uses Bootstrap semantic colour tokens and brighter green links.
- Preserve the recognisable green archive identity while comparing home page structures. Mockups may explore hierarchy and content sequence; they are not approved implementations.
