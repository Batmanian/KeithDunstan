YOU MAY HAVE noted that Sir Henry Bolte was pleased with the Western Australian by-election. The Bulletin noted the revenue figures flowing from alcohol, tobacco, racing and the TAB — collectively hundreds of millions of dollars annually passing through the Federal Treasury — and pointed out the fiscal paradox of the clean-living citizen who, by refusing to drink, smoke, bet or drive, contributes nothing to that stream while his vicious neighbours bankroll hospitals and infrastructure.

The non-drinker, the non-smoker, the non-gambler and the non-motorist was, in this accounting, a burden on the state.
