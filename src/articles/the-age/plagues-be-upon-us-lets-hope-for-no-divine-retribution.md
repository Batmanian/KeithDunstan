THE year 2010 was splendidly wet. We almost doubled the rainfall for 2009. Possums were happy with this: lots more roses and vegetable gardens to eat. So possums went forth and multiplied. Here is an unofficial estimate. There are more possums than humans in Melbourne. Possums are protected under the Wildlife Act of 1975. Humans here have been protected longer than that. They too have multiplied.

Possums indeed are a worry at our place. We had a whole wall of gold bunny roses. They bloomed brilliantly year after year. This year the possums ate the lot. Our local hardware store has been selling plastic owls. Big ones, four times the size of normal owls and they have large menacing eyes that glower.

They are supposed to deter possums and terrify predatory birds. So we acquired some of these owls. Not successful. The possums treat them as friends. The birds sit on them and show their contempt in a particular birdlike manner.

The truth is, we are having a possum plague. We will have to be careful. You will remember the 10 plagues of Egypt. If we go to Exodus, chapter 7, verse 19: "And the Lord spake unto Moses. Say unto Aaron. Take thy rod, and stretch out thine hand upon the waters of Egypt, upon their streams, upon their rivers, and upon all their pools of water, that they may become blood."

This was tough on the Egyptians. All the fish died in the streams and nobody could drink the water. That was just the start of the plagues. Next came frogs. They were everywhere. "Into thy bed chamber... thine ovens and into thy kneading troughs."

After that there was no letup. Lice, flies, livestock diseases, boils, thunder and hail, then came locusts that "shall cover the face of the earth, that one cannot be able to see the earth". The lice were also pretty terrible. "There was lice in man and beast and all the dust in the land became lice through all the land of Egypt."

What else? There was darkness over the land of Egypt, then all the first born had to die.

Well, if God were speaking to Moses about Melbourne and the land of Victoria, we can offer a few plagues also. Locusts, thunderstorms, floods and north winds with 40-degree temperatures come to mind. The fly plague come February is also inevitable, along with the blowies.

There are lots more. The football plague has already started, football scandals, football weddings, football sackings, football transfers, football predictions. Oh yes, football will get into thy bedchamber, into thine ovens, into thy kneading dough. There will be no mercy.

Cars! The car plague has become right out of hand. They have multiplied like the locusts. There are so many, no longer can you see the ground. It is only a matter of months before Melbourne will become one gigantic gridlock of SUVS, CRVs, double Vs and whatnot. The fish will die in the streams from the pollution.

The plague of mobile phones has been particularly severe. Every man, woman and child has been struck with mobile phones. They have rung in the trams, rung in the department stores and even rung in the symphony concerts. All the dust in Melbourne has become mobiles.

The TV crime plague has caused further suffering. Britain, obviously a crime-ridden country, has exported everything from Midsomer Murders through to Frost, Morse, Marple, Dalziel & Pascoe, Cracker, Poirot and The Bill, just to mention some of the kinder of these TV shows. They average three murders an episode and I have counted up to 30 bodies chopped, stabbed, sliced, garrotted and poisoned within one week.

We have to mention also the noise plague. The passion for providing horrid music in restaurants, supermarkets, even in lifts, where the decibels smite the ear right across the Land of Victoria.

Egypt had to suffer eight plagues of locusts, and nine plagues of darkness and the loss of all first-born before the pharaoh would let the Hebrews go. Then our Lord drowned the Egyptian army and all their chariots in the Red Sea.

I don't know what will happen to us, but I am tenderhearted and would like a little mercy for the possums.
