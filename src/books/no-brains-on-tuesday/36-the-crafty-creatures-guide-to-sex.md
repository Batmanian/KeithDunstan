I have nothing against Boy George; he is the prettiest English person you will find in a generation. He is quite wholesome, in his way. His languid pallor is appealing.

It is just that — well — I was brought up in a terribly animal household, and went to a boarding school that was so gently male we couldn't look at the tables. Now the trendy word is androgynous. I can understand all that and I try to think about life on an elegant serene plane. Alas, I cannot.

Am assured a male crocodile knows instantly when he sees a female crocodile. I have a male friend who stood in a queue last week outside a Melbourne Cricket Ground and had been there all morning. It was only afterwards that he realised he had been waiting for the lavatory of the wrong gender.

You used to be able to tell by clothes. Not any more. Boy George has fixed all that. Messrs Chain Stores are tending to that. My daughters love Chanel after-shaves.

You used to be able to tell by shape. Not any more. Two or three females are now bigger and stronger than I am. When I bicycle they bicycle past me. When I jog they jog past me at speed. I did manage to overtake a female last Tuesday but I think she had stopped to do up a shoelace.

You used to be able to tell by their narrow shoulders and their wide hips. Not any more. You see hips that are curiously mirror images. They have shoulders like Mr Barry Round.

Maybe some of you folk out there, like me, are losing your grip, so to speak. Well, I have organised my own information form, a set of clues which enables me to identify male from female.

*Jeans:*

Females wear them shorter up the calf. Furthermore the female of the species can endure much more pain than the male, so it tends to wear the garment much tighter, particularly about the crotch.

*Tops:*

Very sloppy at the moment. This makes it impossible to identify breasts. Both sexes tend to have the just-escaped-from-Afghanistan look. Both sexes tend to wear shirts half unbuttoned. Males are also going for boots and harness at the moment. They are wearing a lot of leather around their middles.

*Sneakers/Joggers:*

Female gym shoes are just slightly more witty. Male sneakers are bigger and dirtier. It is considered right not to wear socks.

*Smoking:*

The females definitely are better at it. If it is a cigarette in the classic little finger teacup style, three to one on it's female.

*Hair:*

Not easy. Both sexes are going for Apaches, lavatory brush styles, spikes, candy colours. However, male hair tends to be more aggressively bleached and longer.

*Ear Rings:*

Very good clue. Most males still are only wearing one ear ring. There are certain messages to be read from which ear, but I am told this is notoriously unreliable.

*Newspapers:*

Males almost invariably start reading the newspaper from the back page. Females tend to talk about sex more, but their conversation is often more intelligent.

*Posture:*

Now this is a delicate subject. I once knew a hostess who said you could always tell where males had been sitting because the crumbs were in the middle of their seats. A lady always kept her knees together.

That knee business doesn't apply any more. I mean, have you noticed, when women are now allowed into clubs and pubs, males do, more often, stand with their legs apart.

*Make Up:*

The female defines herself by doing up her eyes. A male doing his face in public is still unusual.

Not one of these clues that I have given you will prove 100 per cent certain. With all the evidence, I think I can guarantee better than 90 per cent of the time.
