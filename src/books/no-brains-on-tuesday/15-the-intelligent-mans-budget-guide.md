What with this plethora of budgets, Federal Budget last Tuesday, State Budget next Tuesday, it is very difficult to understand money any more or where we are going. We thought it might be helpful to provide our exclusive Economic Dictionary.

**Accord:** Blissful state of Employer-Union harmony, just like Israel with the PLO.

**Back Burner:** Place for putting anything to do with lowering interest rates. (See Front Burner)

**Creep:** Not a term for any Treasurer past or present. It's just how we all creep into a higher tax bracket every time we think of filing a tax return.

**Deficit:** Sometimes we despair as the trade figures come in and we observe our balance of payments but there is always comfort in knowing that running a deficit is our one growth industry.

**Economists:** Ingenious people who know how to solve our problems. They all know how; except history will be made when one economist agrees with another economist.

**Front Burner:** Immediacy with which Government can raise new taxes. (See Back Burner.)

**Gearing:** Charming term for borrowing. You can be highly geared, under geared, even negatively geared, or run entirely out of gears like some of our famous entrepreneurs, Alan, George, Christopher and John.

**Horror:** A technical term usually used to describe budgets.

**Interest:** Never was anything better named. Once you are on to interest rates they get more interesting all the time and in defiance of all laws of motion, what goes up never comes down.

**J-curve:** The tendency to call on the Almighty when one sees the balance of trade figures.

**K is for Keating:** The Treasurer, world's greatest.

**K is for Kirner:** And K is for Kind. The budget, we craveth.

**Liquidity:** State you tend to get into when trying to face up to your own micro economic reform. (See micro)

**Micro:** Economics is just your minor financial disaster. Macro is doing it on a majestic global scale.

**Nuclear Family:** Eventually it all comes back to this. It has virtually no say, it is expected to front in times of war, to be there for every crisis, to shoulder every workload. It is that wonderful group which eventually pays for everything.

**Overseas Aid:** Going into a decline. As a percentage of our national product we are giving less and less. Perhaps it is something required of Australia.

**Pipeline:** Useful word to describe what is going to happen, when your lot is likely to improve. If it is not down the track then at least it is in the pipeline. And here is a bonus P. That is Privatisation. Selling off everything a Government possesses to pay for what it never had.

**Quid:** Could it be that everything started to go wrong when we freed the economy and liberated the dollar? Do you ever yearn back to the days when Ming was there, Artie was Treasurer and a quid was a quid?

**Recession or Economic Downturn:** Useful terms designed to avoid that horrid, vulgar word Depression. And a bonus R is Rubbery, an adjective which marries well with all economic statistics.

**States:** Artificial economies which have to make a journey annually to Canberra to beg for sustenance.

**Tunnel:** A place where you look for a light at the end of, but it could be an engine coming the other way.

**Unemployment:** Curious modern economics, the greater the unemployment, the greater the penury, the better the chance of reducing inflation and lowering interest rates.

**Victoria:** Former colony which is seeking a gold medal for the world's greatest borrowings.

**Wages:** Something which is held down to the correct guidelines as long as you are not a managing director.

**X:** The one area where Canberra is superbly profitable. If it is low on the production of material wealth, nothing beats it as the national capital for X-rated videos.

**Yen:** We are full of yens, the yen for better interest rates, the yen for a non-red balance of payments, the yen for deflation of inflation. But in the end with the rising sun in the north the yen will overtake us all.

**Zero:** State of your bank account when you have paid your interest rates.
